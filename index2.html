PATIENT INFORMATION

A few helpful
answers.

Need more help? Contact the hospital team to confirm current services and schedules.

How do I request an appointment?

Send a routine request using the form on this page. Our team will contact you to confirm availability. Your appointment is not confirmed until you receive confirmation from the hospital.

What should I bring to my consultation?

Bring previous prescriptions, recent investigation reports, a list of medicines you take and any relevant insurance or scheme documents.

Are all departments available every day?

Consultation schedules can vary. Please contact the hospital before travelling to confirm the doctor's schedule and the availability of the service you need.

Can I use this website for urgent medical help?

No. Appointment and enquiry forms are not monitored as emergency channels. For urgent medical needs, seek immediate in-person emergency assistance rather than waiting for a website response.
